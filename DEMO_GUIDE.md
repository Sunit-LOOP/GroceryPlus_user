# GroceryPlus Demo Guide

## 🎬 Demo Script for College Presentation

### Opening (2 minutes)
**Presenter**: "Today I'll be demonstrating GroceryPlus, a complete grocery management system that brings the entire grocery shopping experience to mobile devices."

**Show App Launch**
- Launch app → Show splash screen with loading animation
- Point out professional branding and smooth transitions

---

## 👤 Customer Demo (5 minutes)

### 1. User Registration & Login (1 min)
**Show Login Screen**
- "We start with secure authentication"
- Click "Sign Up" → Show registration form
- Fill in details (name, email, phone, password)
- "Notice the real-time validation and user-friendly interface"
- Complete registration → Auto-login

### 2. Product Browsing (2 min)
**Show Home Screen**
- "This is the customer dashboard with personalized recommendations"
- Scroll through featured products, categories
- "The app learns from user behavior"
- Click on a category → Show filtered products

**Product Details**
- Click on any product → Show detailed view
- Point out product images, description, reviews
- "Customers can see ratings and reviews from other users"
- Click "Add to Cart" → Show success message
- Click "Save for Later" → Show wishlist functionality

### 3. Shopping Cart & Checkout (2 min)
**Cart Management**
- Navigate to cart → Show items
- "Smart cart with quantity controls"
- Update quantities → Show real-time price updates
- "Total calculation includes delivery fees"

**Checkout Process**
- Click "Checkout" → Show address selection
- "Customers can save multiple addresses"
- Select delivery address → Show payment options
- **Highlight**: Stripe integration and Cash on Delivery
- "Multiple payment methods for flexibility"

### 4. Order Tracking (1 min)
**Order Success**
- Complete payment → Show order confirmation
- "Real-time order tracking begins immediately"
- Navigate to Order History → Show placed order
- Click on order → Show live tracking map
- **Key Feature**: "Watch as the delivery boy moves in real-time"

---

## 👨‍💼 Admin Demo (5 minutes)

### 1. Admin Login (30 sec)
**Admin Access**
- Logout → Show login screen
- Use admin credentials (admin@gmail.com / admin123)
- "Role-based access ensures security"

### 2. Admin Dashboard (1 min)
**Dashboard Overview**
- "This is the command center for the entire business"
- Point out key metrics cards
- "Real-time analytics for business decisions"
- Click on Analytics → Show detailed metrics

### 3. Product Management (1.5 min)
**Product Operations**
- Navigate to Product Management
- "Complete inventory control"
- Click "Add Product" → Show product form
- Fill in product details, price, stock
- "Smart stock tracking prevents overselling"
- Add product → Show in list
- Click edit → Show update functionality
- Click delete → Show confirmation dialog

### 4. Order Management (1.5 min)
**Order Processing**
- Navigate to Order Management
- "All customer orders in one place"
- Show pending orders with status indicators
- Click on an order → Show order details
- **Highlight**: "Assign delivery boy with one click"
- Select delivery person → Show assignment success
- Update order status → Show status progression

### 5. Delivery Management (1 min)
**Delivery Personnel**
- Navigate to Delivery Management
- "Smart delivery team management"
- Show delivery boy list with availability status
- Toggle availability → Show real-time update
- "System automatically assigns available delivery boys"
- Add new delivery person → Show form
